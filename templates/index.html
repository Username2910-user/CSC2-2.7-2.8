Our Ethos

History, Flour, and Fire.

In the heart of Naples, where the tradition of pizza-making is alive and well. This city had orginated from a Greek colony, A Roman wonder, A Byzantine legacy, to a Modern Italian culinary Metropolis. Its heart beats with the rhythm of generations and architecture of the mediterranean. The blistering heat from a dome oven fueled by the volcanic fire of Mt Vesuvius, to the wheat grown from generations of farmers that are used to make the dough.The pizza itself was forged to keep the poor of Napoli fed using ingredients both foreign and domestic, to becoming an icon and a symbol of Napolitan culture for centuries to come.
